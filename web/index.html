Audio visualizer

Retro effects driven by your PC's sound. Needs the companion app running with its Audio visualizer option enabled.

Effect

Classic EQ: original bars. Neon Mirror: cyan and magenta pulses. Phosphor Waterfall: scrolling green and amber trails. Purple LED Stage: curved purple and pink LED waves pulsing with the music. Runs until you stop it; returns to normal mode if the audio stream stops for 10s (and resumes when the stream returns).

Game mode

Games

Falling Blocks, Snake, Bricks, Space Rocks, Runner, Defenders, Light Cycles and Frog Crossing, played with an Xbox Wireless Controller over Bluetooth LE (model 1708 or newer, controller firmware 5.x). LED strip

An optional WS2812B strip on a spare GPIO, for a glow under or behind the panel. Feed its 5V and ground from the same terminals as the panels, never through the board's pin header.

Hardware

Drives the strip's DIN through a 330R resistor. Pins the panel, flash, USB or serial port use are refused. On a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero GPIO21 is the onboard RGB LED.

How many WS2812B on the chain.

The strip is dimmed as a whole whenever a frame would draw more than this, so a shared supply is never asked for more than it has. 0 removes the cap. Set it to what is left over after the panels, not to what the strip could take.

Data A 330R resistor and a short data wire are usually enough for the 3.3V GPIO. If the strip flickers or shows wrong colours, fit a level shifter on the data line or drop the strip's 5V through two series Schottky diodes.

Light

Raise it if the bar barely moves on quiet material, lower it if it sits pinned at the ends. Default 100. Heavily compressed music usually wants less.

Display panel

Driver chip and timing for HUB75 panels from other batches. The defaults suit the Waveshare P2.5 panels.

restart
Applying reboots the device. A higher minimum refresh stops flicker but makes the darkest shades brighter. A wrong driver can leave the panel dark - Defaults brings it back.

WiFi transmit power

Applies and saves at once. Some small ESP32-S3 boards transmit worse at full power: if WiFi drops out or the portal loads slowly, try 11-13 dBm. Lower it a step at a time; too little loses range. The tag shows the signal received from the router, which this setting does not change.
